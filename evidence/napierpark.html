People

Our Story

Napier Park formed under the leadership of Jonathan Dorfman and James M. O’Brien. Having worked together for over 25 years, Jim and Jon’s partnership of success began well before Napier Park. They have dedicated their careers to Global Credit Markets. The Firm’s success stems from this deep knowledge and expertise cultivated throughout their partnership together.

They assembled a team who collectively represent decades of experience managing investment teams together. The stability of this team is the nucleus that drives Napier Park’s Culture: Client First, Performance Driven, Integrity and Teamwork.
Managing
Principals

Jonathan Dorfman

Managing Principal and Chief Investment Officer

Jonathan Dorfman

Managing Principal and Chief Investment Officer

Jon Dorfman co-founded Napier Park. Prior to Napier Park, Jon was the Co-CEO & CIO of the alternative investment division within Citigroup: Citi Capital Advisors. He achieved this position through Citigroup’s acquisition of Carlton Hill, a firm he Co-founded with Jim in 2005.

Prior to founding Carlton Hill, Jon spent 20 years leading global investments at Morgan Stanley including Co-Head of Global Investment Grade Credit and Head of Global Credit Derivative and Asset Swap Trading.

Jon has an extensive record of innovation and performance. He was responsible for the first standardized credit default swap contract, a market leader in Credit Indexing (Tracers and CDX) and a pioneer in Credit Market Risk Tranches.

Jon holds a BSE, Magna Cum Laude, from the Wharton School of Business at the University of Pennsylvania.

Jim O’Brien

Managing Principal, Senior Advisor

Jim O’Brien

Managing Principal, Senior Advisor

Jim O’Brien co-founded Napier Park. Prior to Napier Park, Jim was the Co-CEO of the alternative investment division within Citigroup: Citi Capital Advisors. He achieved this position through Citigroup’s acquisition of Carlton Hill, a firm he Co-founded with Jon in 2005.

Preceding Carlton Hill, Jim spent 19 years leading global divisions at Morgan Stanley including Co-Head of Global Corporate Credit, Co-Head of Global Investment Grade Credit, Head of Global Cash Corporate Trading, Head of Global Credit Research and Head of European Corporate Bond and ABS Trading. He was also a member of the US International Sales and Product Management Group.

Jim holds a BS in Finance from The Stillman School of Business at Seton Hall University.
